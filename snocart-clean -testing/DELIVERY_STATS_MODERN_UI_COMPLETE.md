## 📊 New Business Insights

### 6 Insight Cards Added

#### 1. Delivery Success Rate
**Metric:** `(Delivered / Total) * 100`
- Shows percentage of successful deliveries
- Color-coded feedback:
  - 🎉 Excellent (≥90%)
  - 👍 Good (≥75%)
  - ⚠️ Needs Improvement (<75%)
- Shows ratio (e.g., "89 out of 100 orders")

#### 2. Average Order Value
**Metric:** Average order amount today
- Displayed in rupees (₹)
- Performance indicator:
  - 📈 Above target (≥₹300)
  - 📊 Track closely (<₹300)
- Helps track revenue per order

#### 3. Peak Hour Performance
**Metric:** Hour with most orders
- Shows time in 12-hour format (e.g., "2 PM")
- Order count during peak
- Demand indicator:
  - 🔥 High demand (>20 orders)
  - 📊 Regular traffic (≤20 orders)
- Helps optimize staffing

#### 4. On-Time Delivery Rate
**Metric:** Estimated based on avg delivery time
- Algorithm:
  - ≤30 min = 95% on-time
  - ≤45 min = 80% on-time
  - >45 min = 65% on-time
- SLA compliance indicator:
  - ✅ Meeting SLA (≥90%)
  - ⏰ Monitor closely (<90%)

#### 5. Scheduled Orders
**Metric:** Pre-orders for later today
- Shows count of scheduled deliveries
- Planning indicator:
  - 📅 Good planning (>10 orders)
  - 📋 Few pre-orders (≤10 orders)
- Helps anticipate demand

#### 6. Returns Today
**Metric:** Refund requests
- Shows return count
- Quality indicator:
  - ✅ Low return rate (≤2 orders)
  - ⚠️ Monitor quality (>2 orders)
- Tracks customer satisfaction
